Interface

A HUD that shows what matters, when it matters

Every element on screen is there because it earns its place — health, resources and status appear when relevant and fade when they're not.

  • Context-aware elements that fade in and out
  • Reposition, resize or hide any HUD element
  • Colorblind-safe palettes built in
Performance

Tuned to keep frame time low

The rendering pipeline is profiled every release to cut stutter, whether you're on a five-year-old laptop or a current flagship rig.

  • Adaptive quality presets tuned per hardware tier
  • Background asset streaming to avoid load hitches
  • Built-in frame-time overlay for troubleshooting
Make it yours

Customization that goes deeper than a theme picker

From keybind layers to HUD density to accent color, the client adapts to how you play — not the other way around.

  • Layered keybind profiles per game mode
  • Adjustable HUD density and scale
  • Custom accent colors and crosshair styles
Never start from zero

  • Loadouts and keybinds travel with your account
  • Manual snapshots if you want to save a specific setup
